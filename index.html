OUR STORY

Experience you can
see in every detail.

AS Interior Works is built on three decades of hands-on experience in Saudi Arabia’s demanding construction and interior industry. Our founder spent 15 years as a Foreman with Saudi Binladin Group, followed by 6 years with Asean International and 7 years with Saudi Oger.

For the past 5 years, we have been bringing that international experience to Lahore. We have successfully completed residential and commercial work across DHA, Model Town and Johar Town—including custom kitchens, doors, ceilings, partitions and made-to-measure bedroom wardrobes.

Every project receives the same disciplined handling, precise finishing and honest workmanship—from the first measurement to the final installation.
